Elegant Task Management

Things is your elegant personal task manager for macOS. Organize thoughts, projects, and daily plans with intuitive simplicity. Powerful tags and a smart filter bar keep everything effortlessly under control.

Collect Your Thoughts

Capture ideas instantly from anywhere. Use the universal Quick Entry shortcut, Siri on any device, or import from Reminders. Everything lands safely in your Inbox, ready to organize.

Get Organized

Build projects for your goals, add actionable steps, and structure with headings. Group into Areas like Family, Work, or Health. Regular reviews keep you focused and on track.

Plan Your Time

View calendar events alongside to-dos for smart scheduling. Set repeating tasks for routines—Things reminds you precisely when needed, every time.

Make the Most of Your Day

Start mornings with your Today list: review plans, add quick picks from projects, and dive in. It's the single view you need all day.

Customize Your Workflow

Tag for context—Office, Home, Errands, or people like Kate. Filter or search instantly to surface exactly what matters.
